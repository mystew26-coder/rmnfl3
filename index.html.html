RMNFL Rulebook

Season 2026

01 — General Rules

  • All owners are expected to play fairly and respect league members.
  • Games must be played using the approved league Madden settings.
  • Owners are responsible for checking the league platform regularly.
  • Do not intentionally exploit glitches, disconnects or gameplay bugs.
  • Commissioner decisions are final unless overturned by league administration.
  • All official scores must be reported through the league platform.

02 — Trade Rules

  • Trades are subject to a 48-hour review window.
  • Commissioner approval is required before a trade becomes official.
  • Collusion, intentionally unfair trades and roster dumping are prohibited.
  • Trade Block listings are not automatically approved trades.
  • Both owners must agree to the submitted trade.

03 — Scheduling Rules

  • Owners must initiate scheduling within 48 hours of the matchup being posted.
  • Games should be completed within 72 hours.
  • Owners must communicate if they cannot meet the normal window.
  • Repeated scheduling failures may result in discipline.

04 — Discipline Policy

Late Game$5,000
No-Show$10,000
Unsportsmanlike Conduct$25,000

05 — Playoff Rules

  • Playoffs are determined by official league standings.
  • Tiebreakers are applied in league-defined order.
  • Playoff games receive the same scheduling requirements.
  • Commissioner may establish special playoff deadlines.

06 — Madden Sync Rules

  • Owners should sync results after every completed game.
  • Manual score entry may be used when synchronization is unavailable.
  • Owners should report sync errors to administration.
  • Official league records take priority over unofficial screenshots.
