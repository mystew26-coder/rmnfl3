League Rules & Policies

  • All owners must maintain an active roster at all times.
  • Communication is required — check Discord daily.
  • No quitting mid-game. Complete all scheduled matchups.
  • Respect all opponents and league officials.
  • Any exploits, glitches, or cheese plays are strictly prohibited.
  • Commissioner decisions are final on all disputes.
  • All owners must use default game settings unless otherwise noted.
  • Sim-style gameplay is expected. Run realistic plays and formations.
  • All trades must be posted in #trades on Discord for league review.
  • 48-hour trade review window — trades can be vetoed by majority vote.
  • Commissioner approval is required for all trades to be processed.
  • No trades involving future draft picks beyond 2 seasons ahead.
  • Salary cap implications must be considered before trading.
  • Lopsided trades will be reviewed and may be rejected.
  • Trade deadline is Week 12 of the regular season.
  • Games must be scheduled within 48 hours of the week advancing.
  • Scheduled games must be played within 72 hours.
  • If no agreement on time, the commissioner will assign a default slot.
  • Post scheduling info in #scheduling on Discord.
  • Notify your opponent and an admin if you need to reschedule.
  • Failure to schedule results in a warning; repeated offenses lead to fines.
  • $5,000 fine — Late game (not played within 72-hour window).
  • $10,000 fine — No-show (failure to play a scheduled game).
  • $25,000 fine — Unsportsmanlike conduct (trash talk, rage quitting, exploiting).
  • $50,000 fine — Repeat offender (3+ violations in a season).
  • Fines are deducted from in-game salary cap space.
  • Suspensions may be issued for severe or repeated violations.
  • Appeals must be submitted within 24 hours to the commissioner.
  • 7 teams per conference qualify for the playoffs.
  • Division winners receive automatic bids (4 per conference).
  • 3 Wild Card spots awarded by overall record.
  • #1 seed receives a first-round bye.
  • Tiebreakers: head-to-head, division record, conference record, points for.
  • Playoff games must be played within 48 hours — no extensions.
  • Super Bowl will be streamed live on the league's media channel.
  • All owners must download and use the Madden Companion App.
  • Stats must be synced after every game — no exceptions.
  • Failure to sync within 24 hours results in a $5,000 fine.
  • Admin will verify synced data before advancing the week.
  • Report any sync errors immediately in #madden-sync on Discord.
  • Manual score entry is available as a backup if sync fails.
